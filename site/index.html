    Questions

    Are these the official clocks?

    Not yet. They are close approximations, credited to their designers. Official editions are planned with the rights holders.

    How are they made?

    Each clock is modelled and lit in Blender, then rendered in layers. The hands turn live on your screen, with their shadows.

    Which movements are there?

    Station uses Hilfiker's stop-to-go: the seconds hand sweeps in 58.5 seconds and waits at twelve while the minute hand jumps. Bill and Rams tick once a second, like quartz. Jacobsen, Orsay and Ball glide.

    Does the light really change?

    Yes. Station has day, dusk and night lighting and crossfades between them by your local time.

    When can I get it?

    The Mac screensaver comes first. iPhone and iPad follow.
